Build a desktop login page for FOMRA Housing & Infrastructure.

Objective:
Create a premium, modern, enterprise-grade login UI for an HRMS product. The design should feel polished, elegant, and production-ready.

Branding:
- Place the company symbol clearly at the top.
- Show the title: “FOMRA HRMS”.
- Show the subtitle: “Housing & Infrastructure”.

Layout:
- Use a centered login card.
- Include an email field and a password field.
- Add a single blue Sign In button.
- Keep the composition balanced and visually centered.

Visual style:
- White and very light gray background.
- Subtle shadows.
- Rounded corners.
- Clean spacing.
- Sharp, readable typography.
- Minimal but premium corporate appearance.

Design quality:
- Make it look like a 10/10 enterprise HRMS login screen.
- Keep the hierarchy strong and clear.
- Use a modern, polished UI with good contrast and spacing.

Constraints:
- Avoid generic SaaS styling.
- Avoid clutter.
- Avoid oversized empty areas.
- Avoid dull, flat, or template-like design.
- Ensure all text is crisp and easy to read.

Target:
- 16:9 desktop layout
- High-end corporate login screen
